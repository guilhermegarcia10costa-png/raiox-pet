Questions owners ask

Does this help with separation anxiety?

Honest answer: it's not a treatment for separation anxiety — if your dog panics when left alone, a qualified behaviourist is the right call. What it does is give a bored, under-stimulated dog something absorbing to do, and for a lot of dogs that's most of the problem.

My dog destroys everything — will it survive?

The fleece is stitched into a rubber base, not glued. It's for foraging, not chewing — supervise a heavy chewer the first few sessions.

How do I clean it?

Shake out the crumbs, cold machine wash in a laundry bag, air dry. Don't tumble dry.

How long will my dog take?

First few goes, 15–20 minutes. Once they've got the technique, hide the food deeper or freeze a lick mat alongside it.
